# Data from André, Abanades & Flamant 2016 (Renew. Sust. Energy Rev. 64, 703-715, DOI 10.1016/j.rser.2016.06.043)

Read on ScienceDirect (institutional access). Values copied from Tables 2-8 (reference markers removed).
Turning temperatures and enthalpies in Tables 2-5 are thermodynamic values at 1 bar of reacting gas; Table 6 is for 20 % reacting gas;
Tables 7-8 are measured onset temperatures. Enthalpies are given per mole as printed in the paper (basis as in the paper).
