# Data from N'Tsoukpoe et al. 2014 (Applied Energy 124, 1-16, DOI 10.1016/j.apenergy.2014.02.053)

Read on ScienceDirect (institutional access). Values copied from the published tables; reference markers in cells removed, footnote letters kept in brackets.

| File | Paper table |
|---|---|
| table2_candidates.csv | Table 2 - evaluation points for the possible candidates (theoretical) |
| table3_after_dehydration.csv | Table 3 - evaluation after dehydration under water vapour (21 mbar, 105 C) |

Nomenclature (from the paper): dHr reaction enthalpy per mole of salt hydrate; dHr/n per mole of water; dHr,m mass energy density;
dHr,V volume energy density; dHnet net molar energy storage density (after subtracting the evaporation energy); dHnet,V net volume
energy density; eta = dHnet/dHr; * = value including the volume of the water in the water storage tank; rho_s density of the solid.
