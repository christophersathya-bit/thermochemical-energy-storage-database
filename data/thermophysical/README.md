# Material and thermophysical property files

Generated by `python scripts/build_thermophysical.py` (also run by `run_all.py`).
Every value has a source column. **An empty cell means no sourced value exists yet**; nothing is filled in by estimate.

| File | Rows | Content |
|---|---|---|
| `materials_properties.csv` | 80 | One row per storage material: reaction, molar masses, densities (discharged / charged form), Cp at 298 K, thermal-conductivity note, ΔH, ΔS, equilibrium and operating temperatures, energy densities (per kg, per m³ and its basis, per m³ of bed), volume change, deliquescence humidity, cyclability, references, verification |
| `species_properties.csv` | 38 | One row per chemical species: phase, molar mass, density, ΔfH°, S°, Cp(298 K), with source URLs |
| `species_cp_vs_T.csv` | 163 | Cp, S and H−H(298) at 298–1200 K for the 24 species in NIST-JANAF (values copied from the tables) |
| `gas_properties.csv` | 38 | Density, cp, viscosity, thermal conductivity and Pr of N₂ / steam mixtures at 20–600 °C, 1 bar |
| `class_property_ranges.csv` | 7 | Energy density, power, stability and TRL per material class (Kiyabu et al. 2026, Table 4), salt-hydrate conductivity range |
| `cfd_bed_properties.csv` | 11 | Bed and solid properties used in the CFD models, each marked as sourced, derived or assumed |

## Columns of `materials_properties.csv`

| Column | Unit | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `discharged_species`, `charged_species` | – | solid before / after charging (calculated entries) |
| `M_discharged_g_mol`, `M_charged_g_mol` | g/mol | molar mass |
| `density_discharged_g_cm3`, `density_charged_g_cm3`, `density_source` | g/cm³ | crystal density of each form |
| `Cp298_discharged_J_kgK`, `Cp298_charged_J_kgK` | J/(kg·K) | heat capacity at 298.15 K (NIST-JANAF); temperature dependence in `species_cp_vs_T.csv` |
| `thermal_conductivity_W_mK`, `thermal_conductivity_note` | W/(m·K) | material value where sourced; otherwise the class range |
| `dH_kJ_mol_rxn`, `dS_J_molK_rxn`, `dH_kJ_per_mol_gas` | kJ/mol, J/(mol·K) | reaction enthalpy and entropy (charging direction) |
| `T_eq_C`, `T_operating_C`, `T_basis`, `T_release_C` | °C | equilibrium T at 1 bar, characteristic operating T and its basis, heat-release T |
| `ED_grav_MJ_kg`, `ED_vol_GJ_m3`, `ED_vol_basis`, `ED_bed_GJ_m3` | MJ/kg, GJ/m³ | energy densities and what they refer to |
| `volume_decrease_pct` | % | reported volume decrease on dehydration (Kiyabu 2026 Table 5) |
| `volume_change_calc_pct` | % | volume change discharged → charged computed from M and crystal densities |
| `DRH_pct` | % | deliquescence relative humidity at 25 °C |
| `ref_ids`, `verification`, `source_location` | – | references (see `../references.csv`), verification status, page/table |

## Coverage and gaps

* Heat capacity is available for the 24 species in NIST-JANAF. Hydrates, carbonates of Ca/Sr/Ba, Mn oxides and CaH₂ have no Cp yet.
* Material-specific thermal conductivities are not yet sourced; only the salt-hydrate class range (0.3–1.3 W/m/K) is given.
* Densities of 9 hydrate pairs come from Donkers et al. 2017; other crystal densities are handbook values not yet re-read from the source.
