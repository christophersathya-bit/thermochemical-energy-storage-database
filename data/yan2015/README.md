# Data from Yan, Wang, Li, Wang & Fred 2015 (Renew. Sust. Energy Rev. 43, 13-31, DOI 10.1016/j.rser.2014.11.015)

Read on ScienceDirect (institutional access). Tables 1-7 copied as printed (the paper compiles values from its refs [42], [44], [51], [96], [101], [115], [116]).
T* is the turning temperature at which the Gibbs energy of reaction is zero; enthalpies as printed (per mole of the reaction as written).
Note: the COCl2 row is printed as "COCl2(g) <=> CO(g) + 3H2(g)" in the paper (likely a typesetting error for CO + Cl2); copied unchanged.
